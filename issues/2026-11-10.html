The news
Economy: CPI today, December hike odds above 80%

October CPI is due at 6:30am Arizona time. Consensus is +0.3% headline and +0.2% core for the month, down from +0.6% and +0.3% in September. There is no official annual rate this month because of last year’s shutdown gap, though Bank of America estimates about 3.8% headline. Fed Governor Waller said he is leaning toward a December hike, and PPI comes Friday. Stock futures are mixed, and Nvidia is reeling from a board probe of CEO Jensen Huang. GDP is tracking about 3.5%.

Why it matters: A hot print firms up hikes; a cool one eases pressure on stocks and mortgage rates.

Source: CNBC · CNBC yields · Yahoo Finance

Job market: data center hiring still hot

Indeed Hiring Lab data shows data-center postings up 20% in three months through September and 147% above June 2024. One tracker counts 228 open data-center power engineer postings with a US median pay of $155,000 (40 disclosed). Roles hardest to fill include controls (BMS/EPMS), commissioning, and critical facility operations, where marine and military backgrounds are named as strong fits. Examples: a remote Lead Electrical Engineer at Atwell ($100k to $165k, PE required).

Why it matters: Your controls and critical-facility background lines up with the scarcest roles.

Source: Hireoven · Plutarch · Petrogate

Data centers and AI: Google takes over Crusoe’s Wyoming site

Google bought Crusoe’s unfinished Wyoming campus and plans to invest $17 billion. Riot Platforms disclosed a 910 MW, roughly $28 billion lease with Anthropic in Corsicana, Texas, and Anthropic also committed $3.4 billion for 1.7 GW of gas generators. Counter-signals: Fermi’s 222 MW TensorWave lease missed its closing deadline, and Texas now has a Democratic governor-elect who may push a permitting pause.

Source: The AI Insider · Data Center Frontier · Slicast

Mesa housing: now a buyer’s market

Mesa’s single-family median was $482,500 in October, down 3.5% from a year ago. Pending sales fell 48.4%, inventory is up 10.7% to 1,359 homes, and supply is 3.6 months. Mesa’s Cromford Market Index fell to 86.6, a buyer’s-market reading. Metro Phoenix median was $448,000.

Source: ShowingTime · Inside the Valley · AZ Big Media
