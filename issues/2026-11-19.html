Data centers: Arizona power and politics

Phoenix-area projects face transformer lead times up to 160 weeks and medium-voltage switchgear at 52–80 weeks, so energization can slip two to three years. The cost of Arizona’s data center tax break rose 40% to a record $56 million even as new applications pause from July 2026. AG Kris Mayes wants a statewide pause, but Prop 207 could make restrictions costly for local governments.

Why it matters: Power gear, not demand, is the constraint on Phoenix data center work.

Source: AZ Big Media · Arizona Republic via Tucson.com

Mesa housing: deepest buyer’s market since 2022
