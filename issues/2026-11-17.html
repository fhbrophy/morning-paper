Worth a look
Quanta: a 20-year survey finds cracks in the standard model of cosmology

The final Dark Energy Survey results, combined with DESI data, don’t quite fit the leading cosmological model, and dark energy may be changing over time. Rubin Observatory is expected to add billions of galaxies.

Source: Quanta Magazine

IEEE Spectrum: an implant that zaps excess sugar from blood

ETH Zurich’s ECHO device uses 0.75 V electrocatalysis with a glucose monitor to convert glucose to gluconate, which is flushed out in urine. It held mice in the normal range for days. It is far from human use, but the same approach could target other molecules.

Source: IEEE Spectrum

Ars Technica: when will humans actually fly on Starship?

After Starship’s tower catch, Eric Berger estimates a possible crewed lunar landing before 2030 but no Earth landings with people before 2030 and plausibly the mid-2030s. SpaceX appears focused on Starlink and data center satellites first.
