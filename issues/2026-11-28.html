Job market: claims drop to 189,000

Initial claims fell 13,000 to 189,000 for the week ended Nov 21, beating the 200,000 consensus; continuing claims fell 95,000 to 1.697M. October payrolls rose 165,000 and unemployment was 4.1%.

Why it matters: Layoffs remain low.

Source: Dept. of Labor · CNBC

Data centers: big leases, more pushback

Why it matters: Capital keeps flowing while local rules tighten.

Source: DCD · Guardian · SF Standard
