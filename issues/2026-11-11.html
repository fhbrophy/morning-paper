Economy: hot CPI cements a December hike

October CPI rose 0.4% (expected 0.3%) after 0.6% in September, and core rose 0.3% (expected 0.2%). Shelter was +0.3%, energy +0.7%, food +0.5%. There is no official annual rate because of last year’s shutdown gap; estimates run about 3.9% to 4.0%. Fed Governor Waller said a 50-basis-point hike should be considered in December; markets price a 25-point hike as nearly certain, with roughly 13% odds on 50. PPI comes Friday. Nasdaq futures are up this morning after Tuesday’s slide. Job openings rose to 7.52 million in September and layoffs fell.

Why it matters: Source: CNBC · Reuters · FXStreet · AP

CPI is the main driver of rates, stocks and mortgage costs right now.
